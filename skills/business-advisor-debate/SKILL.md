# Business Advisor Debate Skill

## Purpose
Stress-test any marketing, product, pricing, or growth strategy by having two distinct expert personas debate trade-offs from their real-world mental models.

## Required Inputs
- `topic`: The specific business problem or strategy (e.g., "Improving community marketing strategy and reducing churn").
- `advisor_1`: First advisor persona (default: `Alex Hormozi` — focus on Value Equation, Grand Slam Offers, unit economics, high-volume inputs).
- `advisor_2`: Second advisor persona (default: `Russell Brunson` — focus on Hook-Story-Offer, funnels, traffic secrets, community movements).
- `business_context` (optional): Current revenue model, audience, or constraints.

## Debate Structure
1. **Round 1 — Core Diagnosis**:
   - Each advisor diagnoses the real bottleneck in 1 crisp paragraph using their signature frameworks.
2. **Round 2 — Direct Clash & Counterarguments**:
   - `advisor_1` proposes their highest-leverage move; `advisor_2` challenges blind spots and counters with their approach.
   - `advisor_1` responds directly to the critique.
3. **Round 3 — Concrete Playbooks**:
   - Each advisor lays out 3 specific, measurable actions to execute in the next 14 days.
4. **Moderator Synthesis (Best of Both Worlds)**:
   - Clear decision matrix showing where they agree, where they conflict, and a unified **5-Step Action Plan** combining the strongest elements of both perspectives.

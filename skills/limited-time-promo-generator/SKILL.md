# Limited Time Offer (LTO) Promo Generator Skill

## Purpose
Quickly turn any service, product, or backlink/agency promotion into multi-channel Limited Time Offer posts with compelling bonus stacks and clear urgency.

## Required Inputs
- `offer`: What is being promoted (e.g., backlinks, coaching, software deal).
- `bonuses`: Extra value items included during the promo window.
- `deadline_or_scarcity`: Expiration date or limited spot count.
- `cta_url`: Booking, checkout, or DM instruction.

## Output Pack
Generate tailored copy for 4 channels (keeping language simple, punchy, and native to each platform):
1. **Skool / Free Community Post**: Value-led announcement with bulleted bonus stack, clear deadline, and pinned-comment CTA.
2. **LinkedIn Post**: Professional hook + mini case study/reason why + clean bullet stack + CTA in closing line.
3. **YouTube Community Post**: Short, scannable poll or bold announcement (< 600 chars) with direct link.
4. **X (Twitter) Post**: Tight 2–3 line punchy hook + bonus bullets + link.
